Welcome to B.S.P. ITI College

At B.S.P. ITI College (Kanjaulighat, Mathura), we are dedicated to empowering youth with professional technical skills, practical knowledge, and career opportunities. We believe in providing quality industrial training to build a strong foundation for every student.
